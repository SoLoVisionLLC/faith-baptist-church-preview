Who We Are

About Faith Baptist Church

Standing strong on the Word of God, serving Fostoria and Seneca County with the love and truth of the Gospel.

Our Foundation

A Bible-Believing, Gospel-Driven Heritage

Faith Baptist Church was founded on the unchanging truth of the Holy Scriptures. We are an independent Baptist fellowship committed to holding forth the Word of Life in a changing culture.

We use and cherish the King James Bible as God's preserved and authoritative Word for English-speaking people. Our worship is reverent and uplifting, centering on Christ-honoring hymns and verse-by-verse preaching.

Located at 11275 W. Twp. Rd. 116 in Fostoria, Ohio, our congregation welcomes families, seniors, youth, and children from throughout the surrounding community.

Doctrinal Statement

What We Believe

Our core convictions are rooted solely in the authoritative King James Scriptures.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible to be the inspired, infallible, and inerrant Word of God. We faithfully use the King James Bible for all preaching and teaching.

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is the free gift of God by grace through faith in the shed blood and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ, wholly apart from human works (Eph 2:8-9).

The Local Church

The local church is a self-governing assembly of baptized believers observing the biblical ordinances of baptism and communion and carrying out the Great Commission.

Pastoral Leadership

A Message from the Pastor

"Welcome to Faith Baptist Church! Our heart is to point you to Jesus Christ and encourage your family in God's precious Word. Whether you've walked with the Lord for decades or are seeking answers, you will find a warm welcome and genuine Christian fellowship here."

Our pastoral ministry emphasizes clear expository preaching, pastoral visitation, prayer for the sick and burdened, and personal discipleship.

Caring For Your Little Ones

Clean, Safe Nursery for Tots

We understand that young families need a safe and nurturing environment for their infants and toddlers. At Faith Baptist Church, our nursery is staffed by caring, background-checked church volunteers during Sunday morning services.
