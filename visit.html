Common Questions

Visitor FAQs

What is the dress code?

There is no strict dress requirement. You will find people in traditional Sunday dress as well as modest casual attire. We focus on worshipping the Lord together with reverence and joy.

Which Bible version is used?

We use the King James Version (KJV) of the Bible for all preaching and Sunday school lessons. If you do not have a Bible, we are happy to provide one for you to keep!

Where do my children go during the service?

We have a clean, staffed nursery for tots (infants and toddlers) during the 10:00 AM service, as well as Sunday school classes for young children. Teens and adults have Sunday school at 9:00 AM and attend the main service together.

How long does a service usually last?

Our Sunday morning worship service typically lasts approximately 60 to 70 minutes, including hymn singing, prayer, and practical verse-by-verse preaching.
