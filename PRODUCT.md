# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitor is a person or family evaluating Faith Baptist Church before attending, often checking service details on a phone in daylight before driving to a rural church in Fostoria, Ohio. Visitors need to confirm when services happen, where the church is, what is available for children and nursery-age children, and how to call or plan a visit.

## Product Purpose

The site gives visitors a factual, trustworthy path to understand Faith Baptist Church and its complete recurring weekly rhythm, then choose **Plan Your Visit**. Success means a visitor can quickly identify the church, service times, address, children and nursery information, and phone action without encountering invented claims or internal production language.

## Positioning

Faith Baptist Church is **Bible believing. Gospel driven. Growing together in God's Word.** Its visitor experience is grounded in the congregation's real place, confirmed convictions, recurring schedule, and real client photography rather than generic church marketing claims.

## Operating Context

- Public church website for Faith Baptist Church in Fostoria, Ohio.
- Primary use scene: a first-time visitor or family checking service details on a phone before driving to the church.
- Primary action: **Plan Your Visit**.
- Secondary schedule action: **View Service Times**.

## Product Principles

1. Put exact service, location, children, nursery, and call information ahead of decoration.
2. Earn trust with real church photography and confirmed language; never fill factual gaps with plausible copy.
3. Make **Plan Your Visit** the single gentle primary action and keep the complete weekly rhythm easy to find.
4. Keep every public word in the church's visitor-facing voice; production language and former identity never ship.
5. Preserve an auditable path from source facts and media provenance to the deployed artifact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic landmarks, one `h1` per route, logical heading order, skip link, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and minimum 44px touch targets.
- Body text must remain at least 16px; target contrast is at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.
- Honor reduced motion and reduced transparency.
- Reserve stable image dimensions or aspect ratios and use the exact alt text above.
- No horizontal overflow, clipped headings, overlapping text, broken menus, cramped controls, or hidden primary CTA.
- Validate desktop full-page at 1440×1000 (or documented equivalent), true full-page mobile at 390×844 DPR2 from `scrollY=0`, and a secondary 430×932 DPR2 view after fonts and images load.
