# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are first-time visitors, local families, returning churchgoers, and current members in or near Fostoria, Ohio. Their main job is to quickly understand what Faith Baptist Church believes, when it gathers, what is available for their family, and how to visit or contact the church.

## Product Purpose

The website gives Faith Baptist Church a clear, welcoming public home. Success means a visitor can find the main Sunday service, location, phone number, beliefs, children's information, and next step within seconds.

## Positioning

Faith Baptist Church presents itself as Bible believing, gospel driven, and committed to teaching from the King James Bible. The site should express an energetic American red, white, and blue identity while keeping the church, Scripture, and local community at the center.

## Operating Context

The church gathers for Sunday School for adults and teens at 9:00 AM, a main service at 10:00 AM, an evening service at 6:00 PM, and Wednesday prayer and Bible study at 7:00 PM. Young children's Sunday School and nursery care for tots are available during Sunday programming.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript keep the site inexpensive and easy for a solo operator to maintain.
- The site calculates the next weekly gathering in the visitor's browser.
- The public address is 11275 W. Twp. Rd. 116, Fostoria, Ohio 44830.
- The public phone is 419-348-2171.
- Leadership, history, accessibility, event, giving, email, sermon, social, and livestream details remain open until the church supplies them.
- Unsupported claims and simulated service integrations must not appear as production features.

## Brand Commitments

- Official name: Faith Baptist Church.
- Required palette direction: American flag white, red, and blue.
- Required visual character: dynamic, exciting, creative, and intentionally not boring.
- Supplied assets include a red, white, and blue church crest plus real sanctuary and exterior photography.
- The American theme must remain welcoming, nonpartisan, and centered on Christian faith.

## Product Principles

- Make the first visit feel easy and understandable.
- Put confirmed church truth ahead of decorative claims.
- Let real local photography carry trust.
- Keep Plan Your Visit as the dominant public action.
- Preserve a clear replacement list for every open content decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must preserve semantic landmarks, logical heading order, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, and layouts without horizontal overflow on common phone widths.
