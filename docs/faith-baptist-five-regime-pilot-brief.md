## Variant A - No Design Skill - Plain Welcome

### Regime Receipt

- Regime: Quill craft judgment only.
- Required receipt text: Design regime A: no design skill used.
- Prohibited inputs: Taste, Frontend Design Pro Demo, UI/UX Pro Max, Impeccable, design search scripts, theme lookups, and generated token systems.

### Visual Thesis

A familiar church website made exceptionally clear. The visual language comes directly from the real building: brick red, white steeple, navy wayfinding, green lawn, rectangular geometry, and quiet spacing. It should feel handmade for this congregation rather than styled by a trend library.

### Tokens and Craft Rules

- Palette: brick #7A302B, navy #18324B, chapel white #FFFFFF, paper #F5F3EE, lawn #426A43 used only as a small support color.
- Typography: Georgia for restrained headings and the system sans-serif stack for body copy. No downloaded display font and no mixed-style word emphasis.
- Geometry: square image crops or a single 4px radius; rectangular buttons; one-pixel rules; no pills, blobs, glass, bento tiles, glow, gradients, or decorative icon system.
- Motion: none beyond native focus, hover, and menu state. No scroll reveal.
- Wordmark: text-only Faith Baptist Church. Do not fabricate an official mark.

### Home Composition

1. Simple 72px header with text wordmark, six links, and a plain Plan Your Visit button.
2. Landscape exterior hero. Keep the photograph legible. Place Faith Baptist Church, the exact identity line, and two actions in a compact lower-left panel.

4. Two-column welcome block: short client-facing invitation on the left, address and phone on the right.
5. Three literal visitor pathways: Service Times, Children and Nursery, Directions. Use text and rules, not generic cards.
6. Sanctuary close image beside the three confirmed beliefs.
7. Four ministry rows with times and brief factual descriptions.
8. Full-width sanctuary wide image.
9. Address, phone, directions, and Plan Your Visit close.

### Inner Pages and Mobile

### Visual Thesis

A decision-friendly Sunday planner, not a sales funnel. The home page pairs one strong real image with a concise schedule and lets each visitor answer four practical questions: when, where, what about children, and how to call.

### Taste System Commitments

- One page theme: light by default with a coordinated dark preference mode, never section-by-section theme inversion.
- One accent: brick #A24532. Neutral family: slate, not mixed warm and cool grays.
- Typography: self-host Source Sans 3 or use the existing project sans if already available. Do not default to Inter. Use weight and scale rather than a second display family.
- Radius rule: 12px content surfaces, 8px controls, no pills except the mobile menu toggle if technically necessary.
- Shadows only where elevation communicates navigation or a floating schedule panel. Tint shadows toward navy.
- No em dash or en dash in visible copy. No three equal feature cards. No section-number eyebrows, scroll cues, decorative status dots, overlaid photo labels, or repeated uppercase eyebrows.
- Primary CTA wording remains Plan Your Visit everywhere. Do not introduce a duplicate contact-intent label.
- Motion stays static except 150-200ms hover, active, focus, and menu transitions. No automatic entrance motion. Reduced motion still disables nonessential transitions.
